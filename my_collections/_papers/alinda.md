A month-long global campaign of MIT observers distributed across three telescopes at Wallace Astrophysical Observatory (WAO), USA and three telescopes at Teide Observatory on the island of Tenerife, Spain determined that 887 Alinda has a synodic period of 73.96 ± 0.01 hours and a lightcurve amplitude of 0.23 ± 0.01 magnitudes as measured at a distance of approximately 12.2 million km from Earth.
